## Acceptance gate — page 20, adapted to this portfolio

For a substantial portfolio revision, record pass, gap, not applicable or unverified with evidence for:

1. Name/identity is clear on arrival; role and intended collaboration are understandable.
2. Contact is easy to find and uses a working, confirmed destination.
3. Portrait is clear and consistent with the approved real/drawn placements.
4. Credibility is factual, accurately qualified and supported.
5. Homepage work is curated and reachable; key identity and selected work can be scanned without leaving the homepage.
6. Thumbnails/mockups communicate the real project in context without competing text or poor crops.
7. Case studies lead with outcomes, describe role/process and remain skimmable.
8. Mobile/short-window layouts, keyboard paths and reduced motion remain usable.
9. The personal domain is documented and checked when deployment is in scope. Never call local readiness deployed.
10. The next step is clear at the end of the page.

For bounded corrections, test the affected criteria and preserve earlier evidence; don’t claim a fresh whole-site pass. Browser inspection is required for visual/interaction claims. Builds and source review are supporting evidence, not substitutes.
